Pokémon card placeholder generator

This tool generates a list of Pokémon, with their national Pokédex number & sprite, designed for printing out to use as placeholders in a card binder.

Made with love for Jupe, my partner, who is currently compiling a binder of their favourite card for every Pokémon.

The printable stylesheet for this enforces a 3-column layout, which gets 9 cards per portrait A4 sheet of paper. I haven't even tried to see what it does when printing to US Letter paper - you're on your own there.

Pokédex data + Pokémon sprites courtesy of PokéAPI.
